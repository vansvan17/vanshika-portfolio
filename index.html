The short version

I like building interactive things, but not everyone has time to explore a portfolio. Here is the short version:

  • I build sensor units: circuit and PCB design, analog, sensors on Raspberry Pis, firmware, and the dashboard on top.
  • I designed a 6-layer hardware security module: STM32, secure element, tamper detection, isolated power, secure boot and encrypted USB-C.
  • I write low-level C++: a lock-free queue, a sharded allocator, an epoll HTTP server, a vectorized query engine and a small JIT compiler.
  • My fixes are merged into Qualcomm's eld linker and spdlog.
  • ICPC 2026 Asia regionalist (Amritapuri), Codeforces Expert with a max of 1604, and 1000+ problems solved.
  • I built a 24-week offensive security course, from web hacking down to binary exploitation and fuzzing.
  • I research real-time scheduling, concurrent data structures and heaps.
  • I take on small, clearly scoped backend, data, integration, and reliability engagements.

About

I'm Vanshika, a CS student who likes the parts of computing where tradeoffs are real: concurrency, databases, real-time systems, security, and hardware.

I study Computer Science at BITS Pilani (B.Sc., expected 2029) and am based in Bengaluru, India.

I learn best by building close to the metal, measuring behaviour, finding the failure mode, and then making the design simpler. I'm currently looking to grow through systems-oriented internships, open source, and carefully scoped engineering work.
